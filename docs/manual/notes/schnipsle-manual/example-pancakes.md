# Example: Pancakes

An example note of the [Schnipsle manual](schnipsle-manual.md): a recipe with a table, a numbered list and tags. Change it, add tags, or delete it; it is yours.

For about eight thin pancakes:

| Ingredient |      Amount |
| :--------- | ----------: |
| flour      |       200 g |
| milk       |      400 ml |
| eggs       |           3 |
| salt       |     a pinch |
| butter     | for the pan |

1. Whisk the flour, milk, eggs and salt into a smooth batter, and let it rest for **20 minutes**.
2. Melt a little butter in a pan over medium heat.
3. Pour in a ladle of batter and tilt the pan until the bottom is covered.
4. Turn the pancake when its edge comes loose, after about a minute, and bake the other side.

> Serve with jam, or with sugar and lemon juice.
