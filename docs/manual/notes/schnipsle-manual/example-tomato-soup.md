# Example: Tomato soup

An example note of the [Schnipsle manual](schnipsle-manual.md): a recipe with tasks and a footnote.

## To buy

- [ ] 1 kg ripe tomatoes
- [ ] 1 onion
- [ ] 2 cloves of garlic
- [x] olive oil
- [x] salt and pepper

Tap a box to tick it. The search `has:task` finds notes with tasks, and `has:task #schnipsle_manual/recipes` the recipes among them.

## Cooking

1. Chop the onion and the garlic, and soften them in olive oil.
2. Add the tomatoes, cut in quarters, and simmer for _25 minutes_.[^1]
3. Purée the soup, and season it with salt and pepper.

[^1]: Tinned tomatoes work too, and need only 15 minutes.
