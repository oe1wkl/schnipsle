# Schnipsle manual

How to keep notes in Schnipsle: plain Markdown files in a folder of your own, organised by tags and saved searches.

- [Getting started](getting-started.md)
- [Query language](query-language.md)
- [Music](music.md)
- [Keyboard and VoiceOver](keyboard.md)

Each part is a note of its own, in the folder `schnipsle-manual` of your vault. Try things out on them and change them as you like; **Help › Manual** on the Mac, or **⚙︎ › Open the manual** on the iPhone, brings you back to this page.
